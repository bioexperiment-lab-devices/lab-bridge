The agent installer is a fresh Windows binary that doesn't yet carry a trusted publisher signature or any reputation in Microsoft Defender SmartScreen. If your browser hides the download (Edge, Chrome, Brave):

  1. Open the downloads list (Ctrl+J).
  2. Find SerialHop-v{{ info.version }}.exe, click the ⋯ menu (or right-click) and choose Keep.
  3. If a second prompt warns "This file might harm your computer", choose Keep dangerous file.

When you double-click the .exe and Windows shows "Windows protected your PC":

  1. Click More info in the SmartScreen dialog (the "Run anyway" button is hidden until you do).
  2. A Run anyway button appears beneath the text — click it.
  3. Windows remembers the choice on this PC, so the prompt won't come back on later launches.
